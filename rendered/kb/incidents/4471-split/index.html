How each report was placed

  1. One report per id. The ticket system exports in overlapping pages, so some reports come twice; every copy of a report moves with it.
  2. The desk’s one-day notes go first. A report that says the date moved by one day did not get a flipped month, whatever its tenant.
  3. Every other report goes by its tenant and the way it came in, as causes.ts runs it: a form report from a day-first tenant on booking-v3 is hint, the rest is parser.
  4. Intake’s tag is not used. It is a guess from the reporter’s browser, and the table’s “tagged as” column shows how often it names the other cause.

Every body was read. Apart from the one-day reads, each report under parser or hint either swaps day and month, or gives no date to check against.

What changes for the desk

  • parser is what #4471 already describes, and its queued fix stays the right fix for these reports.
  • hint survives that fix. booking-v3’s form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, so people at day-first tenants type the month first, and the form reads their input day first. The form needs the tenant’s own order under its date field, as booking-v2 already does.
  • one-day is a third bug: the date lands a day early or late, and one report has the same meeting on different days in Lisbon and Toronto. Look at how the date crosses time zones on its way into the invite.
  • Decision 0001 merges on subject alone, so reports with the same subject and different causes ended up in one ticket. A new decision record should replace 0001; this page does not edit it.
